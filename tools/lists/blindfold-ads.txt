! over class names: Ad Shield removes the classes a hide rule matched, and the

! --- Yahoo (www and news; the markup below was checked on those two) ------------
! Billboard: <div class="cls-ad-top-center ... md:min-h-[250px]"> holding
! .ad-container boxes, each with an empty #top_center-<page>_<width>.
www.yahoo.com,news.yahoo.com##div:has(> div > [id^="top_center-"])
! Right rail top unit: <div class="cls-ad-rail flex"> > .ad-container > #top_right-*.
www.yahoo.com,news.yahoo.com##div:has(> div > [id^="top_right-"])
! Sticky rail: <div class="cls-ad-rail ad-container min-h-[607px]"> > div > #sda-*.
! On news.yahoo.com the same #sda-* boxes share a sticky column with the
! footer, which must stay.
www.yahoo.com,news.yahoo.com##div:has(> div > [id^="sda-"]):not(:has(#footer))
! Stream native ad: <li class="cls-stream-ad ..."> with skeleton boxes and an
! empty #taboola-stream-fp-<n>.
www.yahoo.com,news.yahoo.com##li:has(> [id^="taboola-stream-"])

! --- Future plc (TechRadar, Tom's Guide) ------------------------------------------
! <div data-widget-type="ads" id="ads-lightbox2"> holding only an empty
! #bdx_static-lightbox2; site CSS reserves 250 to 350 px for it.
techradar.com,tomsguide.com##div[data-widget-type="ads"]
! Homepage listing grid: an ad cell is <div class="listingResult small result2">
! holding only <div class="sponsored-post"><div></div></div>. EasyList hides the
! inner div, which leaves the grid cell (about 297x404 on tomshardware.com)
! empty. Same technique as AdGuard's tomshardware/techradar rules; :only-child
! keeps a real story that also carries a sponsored-post child.
tomshardware.com,techradar.com,tomsguide.com,gamesradar.com##.listingResult:has(> .sponsored-post:only-child)

! --- Reuters ----------------------------------------------------------------------
! Class names as Safari rendered them (CSS-module prefixes; the page is behind a
! bot wall for curl): the 250 px grey band above the masthead and the reserved
! right-column story-cluster item.
reuters.com##div[class*="leaderboard-space-module__lead"]
reuters.com##li[class*="story-cluster-module__ad-reser"]

! --- Dailymotion ------------------------------------------------------------------
! 500 ms after render the app checks a bait <div class="ad_box">: if EasyList's
! generic .ad_box rule collapsed it, it shows the "Ad blocker detected" popin over
! the page. The timer's callback calls adBlockerDetection(); defuse that timer
! (AdGuard does the same).
dailymotion.com#%#//scriptlet('prevent-setTimeout', 'adBlockerDetection')

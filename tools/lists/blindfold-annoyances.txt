! --- Mobile gaps found by the iOS sitecheck (2026-10-23) ---------------------------
! cnn.com: WBD UserConsent's US "Legal Terms and Privacy" acknowledgment modal
! (role=dialog inside an unclassed fixed backdrop, z-index 999999) covers the
! page on phone and desktop and freezes the body with inline position:fixed
! and overflow:hidden. Hidden here (the terms are not acknowledged for the
! user); cosmetic.js frees the stranded freeze once it has hidden the backdrop.
cnn.com#?#div[role="dialog"][aria-modal="true"]:has-text(/^\s*Legal Terms and Privacy/):upward(1)

weather.com##div[data-testid="get-app-button"]

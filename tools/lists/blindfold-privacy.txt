! Title: Blindfold privacy rules
! Blindfold's own fixes for the Privacy content blocker, merged by
! tools/build_lists.py after EasyPrivacy and Peter Lowe's list. An exception
! here only cancels blocks in the Privacy list; fixes for the Ads or
! Annoyances blockers go in their own files.

! --- CBS Sports video (Tealium) ------------------------------------------------
! Peter Lowe's list blocks all of tags.tiqcdn.com. CBS Sports' /watch/ video
! pages build their player only after the site's Tealium container
! (utag.js) has loaded; with it blocked the page shows the poster and no
! player. Only CBS Sports' own container path, only on cbssports.com: the
! trackers that container loads stay blocked by their own rules.
@@||tags.tiqcdn.com/utag/cbsi/cbssportssite/$script,domain=cbssports.com

! --- CBS Sports live (Google DAI stream) -------------------------------------
! /watch/live (CBS Sports HQ) is a Google Dynamic Ad Insertion stream: the
! IMA DAI SDK creates the stream with a fetch to
! pubads.g.doubleclick.net/ssai/event/<asset>/streams, and the HLS playlists
! it returns are the only source the player gets. Peter Lowe's list blocks all
! of doubleclick.net; EasyList's @@||pubads.g.doubleclick.net/ssai/ cancels
! that only inside the Ads blocker, so the player said "An error occurred.
! Please try again later." Only the stream-creation fetch, only on
! cbssports.com: the ad and tracking calls on doubleclick.net stay blocked.
@@||pubads.g.doubleclick.net/ssai/$xmlhttprequest,domain=cbssports.com
